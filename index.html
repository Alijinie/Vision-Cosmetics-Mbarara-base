Our vision and mission

Vision statement

To be the leading manufacturer of skin, facial, hair and nail care brands in Uganda, East Africa and beyond — offering high-quality, competitive and results-driven products to our customers.

  • Innovative products that are safe to use
  • Consumer feedback at the centre of what we do
  • Youth employment and professional development for our people

Mission statement

Vision Cosmetics is dedicated to “creating a beautiful world” through research and the development of high-quality, effective products that promote, restore and maintain hair and skin health, nail care and beauty — for a more beautiful you and me.
